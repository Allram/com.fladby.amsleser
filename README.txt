See what your home uses right now, straight from the HAN port of the smart meter. The AMS reader becomes the main meter in Homey Energy, shows power and current per phase, and keeps track of today's imported and exported energy, also when solar panels send power back to the grid.

This app is not made by or affiliated with Utilitech AS, the maker of the AMS reader. We made it because we missed it in Homey.
