See your home's power use live in Homey. The app reads the AMS reader (amsleser.no) on your local network and shows power, imported and exported energy, voltage and current per phase, and what you have used this hour and today.

The reader becomes the main meter in Homey Energy. With solar panels, power shows as negative while you export, and you see both imported and exported energy per day.

Flow cards let you react when this hour's usage passes a limit, when the capacity step changes, or when the reader loses contact with the meter.

Readers on the same network are found automatically. You can also add one by IP address.

This app is not made by or affiliated with Utilitech AS or amsleser.no. We made it because we missed a Homey app for the AMS reader.
